    The first upload gets the views.

    Your biggest moments already get clipped, by clip pages and campaign clippers who post them to their own accounts hours later. By then the moment has been reposted thirty times and the algorithm has moved on.

    Chat Lost It works off the live stream, not the VOD. Your clip exists while the moment is still trending in chat, on X and in Discord, so the first version people see comes from you.

    What happens while you stream

    Nothing to install, no bot in your chat. We work from your public stream and chat, and keep the last 15 minutes of video ready to cut.

    1. 1

      Read the room

      Every 5 seconds we score how hard chat is going: messages per second, emote spam like KEKW, OMEGALUL and "CLIP IT", viewer-made Twitch clips, and how loud the stream just got.

    2. 2

      Catch the spike

      When the score crosses the clip line, we let the moment play out for 30 seconds, then cut it straight from the live buffer.

    3. 3

      Judge it

      AI reads the transcript, the chat and frames from the video, then scores the moment. Weak ones are dropped. Keepers are trimmed so the payoff hits in the first few seconds.

    4. 4

      Make it postable

      A 1080×1920 video with facecam stacked over gameplay, word-by-word captions, a hook on screen in the first 2.5 seconds and audio leveled for phone speakers.

    5. 5

      Approve and post

      Your phone buzzes. One tap approves it, with titles, captions and hashtags already written for TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

    Questions

    Do I need to install anything or add a bot?

    No. We work from your public stream and chat. Nothing runs on your PC and nothing joins your chat.

    Which platforms do you support?

    Twitch and YouTube Live, using chat and audio to find moments. Kick works with audio only for now. Clips are made for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

    Who posts the clips, and where?

    Your call. Clips can go to your own accounts, to a dedicated clip channel for you, or both. Every clip credits your channel either way.

    Can I see clips before they go out?

    Yes. Every clip waits for approval. You can approve them yourself from your phone, hand that to your team, or let us curate to rules you set.

    What if I want it to stop?

    Tell us and we stop recording your channel within a minute. No contract during or after the free month.

    What does it cost after the first month?

    A flat monthly rate based on how much you stream, agreed on a call before the free month ends. No per-clip fees.
